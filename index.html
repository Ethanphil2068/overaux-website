Our Story

A chill zone in the City of Smiles

OverAux The Chillin' Zone is a homegrown Bacolod café built around one simple idea: a place where you can slow down. Come for a thirty-peso cup of Arabica in the morning, stay for loaded nachos and smash burgers at dusk, and cap the night with signature drinks and friends.

With cozy indoor and outdoor seating, accommodating staff, and a menu that spans comfort food to cocktails, it's no wonder thousands of Bacolodnons have made OverAux their hangout — over 1,700 check-ins and counting.
